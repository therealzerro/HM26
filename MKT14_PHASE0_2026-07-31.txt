PHASE 1 PRE-QUESTION — THE AD KIND (report, not decide)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A precedent already exists, and it is stronger than datedChipExcluded:
MKT-21/MKT-07 ruled the slate stamp SUPPRESSED for generated/evergreen
bodies — an evergreen body has no day for the chip to agree with, so
stamping it manufactures exactly the MKT-18 false-claim class.
=> Under an IN-STAMP design the ad kind receives NO in-body attribution at
all; its only wordmark is the endcard close. datedChipExcluded is a separate
mechanism (the identity chip) resting on the same principle. If attribution
on the ad lane matters, that argues for a SECOND mechanism there — not for
changing this one.
