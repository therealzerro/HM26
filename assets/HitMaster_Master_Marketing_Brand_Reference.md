# 1. SITUATIONAL CONTEXT & BACKGROUND

## The product

HitMaster ZK6 is a data intelligence and analytics platform launching
soon on App Store and Google Play. The product applies statistical
pattern recognition methodologies to publicly available numerical data —
functionally analogous to:

- ESPN Stats (sports analytics)
- FiveThirtyEight (political/demographic analytics)
- Yahoo Finance (market analytics)
- Bloomberg (financial intelligence)

The product happens to analyze lottery patterns, but the brand positions
as a software analytics platform first.

## The audience

- 14K followers on the main HitMaster Facebook page
- Free Facebook community group (linked in bio)
- Paid Pro community group ($0.99/month subscription)
- Cross-section: serious data-driven players + curious followers +
  prospective subscribers waiting on the app launch

## The constraint

The Facebook page was de-recommended by Meta in May 2026 due to
algorithmic misclassification as gambling-adjacent. After two denied
appeals, the page was restored to recommendation eligibility on
May 17, 2026 — likely due to content rehabilitation work, platform-
wide classifier recalibration, or both.

The page is now in a **probationary period**. Reverting to old voice
will almost certainly trigger re-flagging.

# 2. THE FACEBOOK RECOMMENDATION INCIDENT & RECOVERY

## What the original notification meant

> "We can't recommend your Page. Our Recommendations Guidelines are
> designed to maintain a higher standard than our Community Standards,
> because recommended content and connections are from accounts or
> entities that you haven't chosen to follow."

The phrase "entities that you haven't chosen to follow" refers to
Meta's OTHER users (the strangers Facebook would otherwise show the
page to). It does NOT mean HitMaster needs to follow more pages/groups.

## What the restriction affected

- ❌ "Pages You May Like" placements
- ❌ "Suggested for You" appearances in non-followers' feeds
- ❌ Algorithmic recommendation in search and Explore
- ❌ Inclusion in Facebook's recommendation widgets

## What the restriction did NOT affect

- ✅ Posts continued to show up in existing followers' feeds
- ✅ Posting, commenting, sharing all worked normally
- ✅ Direct search discovery still worked
- ✅ Group posting was unaffected

## Why HitMaster got flagged

Meta's recommendation classifier is stricter than Community Standards
and treats anything in the orbit of gambling, lottery, or games-of-chance
as restricted by default — even when the page is not actually running one.

The classifier sees these signals as gambling-adjacent:
- Posts showing specific winning numbers
- "Daily Heat Picks" framing
- "Hits" terminology with counts
- Brand imagery with lottery-style numerical content

Once classified, recommendations are removed.

## Meta's policy (verbatim from research)

> "Pages that promote content related to gambling, games of skill,
> lotteries, sports betting, bingo, poker, and online casinos are not
> allowed unless Facebook has given you prior authorization."

> "Common types of gambling [requiring authorization]: lotteries,
> raffles, casinos, fantasy sports, bingo, and poker."

## The five recovery options (ranked by ROI)

### Option 1 — File the appeal

Settings → Page Status → Extra Features → Recommendations → request review.

Choose: **"I think the technology misunderstood my intent or the
context of my posts."** (NOT the "guidelines should change" option.)

Cost: 5 minutes. Success rate: ~30% historically. Worth attempting but
not the durable fix.

### Option 2 — Restructure page content (the real fix)

The long-term play. To get recommendations restored, the page needs
to LOOK like a data/analytics brand to the algorithm.

Specific changes:
- Stop posting specific 3-digit numbers in image captions and text
- Reframe "hits" → "patterns identified," "signals matched"
- Replace "Daily Heat Picks" → "Daily Intelligence Drop," "Signal Report"
- Lead with technology, not outcome
- Audit profile photo and cover photo — minimize lottery iconography
- Remove "lottery" from About section

### Option 3 — Make the free group the primary growth engine

Groups have different discovery mechanisms than pages. Post specific
numbers INSIDE the group (members opted in), keep the public page
abstract.

### Option 4 — Age-restrict the page (18+)

Settings → Page Status → Age Restrictions → 18+. Meta's classifier
looks for age-gating on gambling-adjacent content.

**Important caveat**: Adding an age restriction removes the page from
any groups it is currently part of. Audit before flipping.

### Option 5 — Long-term brand split

Consider whether the public Facebook brand should diverge from the
product brand. The app stays HitMaster ZK6; the public page becomes
something more abstract ("ZK Intelligence," "Pattern Engine").
Nuclear option — don't pursue before trying 1-3.

## Status as of May 17, 2026

- Two appeals filed, both denied
- Page restored to recommendation eligibility without third appeal
  (likely automatic re-evaluation due to content shift)
- Current state: **probationary**
- Monitoring period: 30+ days of rehabilitated voice to lock in the
  reclassification

These trigger Meta's gambling classifier. Do not use in public-facing
graphics, video text, captions, ad copy, or alt text.

- "lottery" / "lotto" / "Pick 3"
- "winning numbers" / "winners" / "winning"
- "daily picks" / "hot picks" / "heat picks" / "today's picks"
- "hits" (as a count of correct predictions)
- "play" / "play smart" / "play the numbers"
- "gamble" / "gambling" / "bet" / "betting"
- "lucky" / "luck" / "fortune"
- "jackpot" / "payout"
- "get rich" / "easy money" / "guaranteed wins"

### Forbidden visual elements

- Specific 3-digit numbers in prominent positions
- Cash, dollar bills, money fans, stacks of bills
- Lottery tickets, scratch-off cards
- Casino imagery (dice, cards, chips, roulette, slot machines)
- "WINNER" graphics, confetti-over-money compositions
- Dollar signs as decorative elements

### Forbidden tone elements

- Hype-y, aggressive, urgency-screaming language
- "Don't miss out!" / "Last chance!" / "Act now!"
- Caps-lock dominance
- Excessive emoji use (more than 3 per post is too many)

# 4. DESIGNER AGENT SKILL BRIEF

This is the operating rulebook for any designer agent (graphic, video,
or copy) working on HitMaster ZK6 public-facing content.

## Internal vs external content rule

Content posted INSIDE the private free group or paid Pro group can use
the original "picks" voice — members have opted in. Apply the
restrictions ONLY to:

- Public Facebook page posts
- Public Instagram posts
- Reels, TikToks, public videos
- Paid ads (additional Meta restrictions apply)
- Cover photos, profile photos, page banners
- App Store listing, push notifications, share previews

## Visual design guidelines

### Strict palette

- **Primary**: Electric purple (#A855F7 to #C084FC)
- **Accent**: Metallic gold (#FBBF24 to #F59E0B)
- **Background**: Deep black (#0A0A0F) to deep navy (#1E1B4B)
- **Highlight**: Pure white, electric cyan for data accents
- Use the lightning bolt motif in every brand-anchored asset

### Typography

- Bold geometric sans-serif for headlines and brand wordmark
- Monospace for data displays, status indicators
- Thin elegant sans-serif for narrative or quote text

### Approved imagery direction

- Analytics dashboards (charts, signal waves, status panels)
- Data streams (cascading numbers as ambient particles, NOT specific picks)
- Holographic displays, futuristic UI, glowing data nodes
- Construction/build aesthetic ("Something powerful is being built")
- Real people using technology (focused expressions, calm confidence)
- Lifestyle aesthetic — clean desks, focused work
- Cosmic/portal imagery for "intelligence is descending" metaphors

### Forbidden imagery direction

- Cash close-ups, money rain, dollar bill compositions
- Casino floors, dice mid-roll, cards spreading
- Lottery ticket close-ups, scratch-off reveals
- "Winner" stock photography with money props
- Slot machine imagery
- Anything that could appear in a casino ad

## Pre-delivery quality check

Before submitting any asset:

- [ ] No specific 3-digit numbers visible in caption text or graphic text
- [ ] No use of forbidden words
- [ ] No cash, casino, dollar, or slot machine imagery
- [ ] Brand palette preserved
- [ ] Lightning bolt motif present (when brand-anchored)
- [ ] Vocabulary lands in "data intelligence" zone
- [ ] Tone is calm/confident, not hype-y
- [ ] CTA points to community/group/follow, not to numbers
- [ ] Spell-check pass complete (history of typos like "Sccoguition")
- [ ] Sanity check: Could ESPN Stats reasonably produce this?

## Edge cases

### When the client requests something on the forbidden list

> "This element is on the rehabilitation forbidden list — Meta's
> classifier reads it as gambling-adjacent and will reinforce the
> page de-recommendation. Here's a reframed version that delivers
> the same message in the data-intelligence voice: [proposed alternative]."

Always offer an alternative. Never just refuse.

### When in doubt

Lean toward the more abstract, more tech-coded version. The default
during rehabilitation should always be "more analytics, less lottery."

# 6. CROSS-POSTING STRATEGY

When posting from HitMaster's free group INTO other groups, different
etiquette applies. You're a guest, not the host.

## Cross-post caption — admin-respectful version

> 🙏 Posting with permission to share value with this community.
>
> If you're an admin or member of a Pick 3 group, we'd like to bring
> more to the table. Our free community delivers data intelligence
> reports, verified pattern analysis, and cross-jurisdictional insights
> that complement what you're already doing.
>
> No fees. No hype. Just intelligence.
>
> 👇 Tap join if it adds value for your crew.

## Tactical rules for cross-posting

- **Always check the group rules first** — many groups forbid self-promo
- **"Posting with permission" only works if it's true** — DM admins first
- **Lead with value, not the ask** — emphasize what THEY get
- **Don't post identical captions in every group on the same day** —
  Facebook's spam classifier picks this up and shadow-suppresses reach
- **Engage with comments on each cross-post** — quality over quantity
- **Space the cross-posts out** — 2-3 groups per day max, varied captions
- **Watch the classifier risk** — rapid identical posts to many
  lottery-related groups is exactly the pattern that triggers re-flagging

# 9. VIDEO PRODUCTION GUIDELINES

## Format

- 9:16 vertical primary (Reels, TikTok, Stories) — 1080×1920
- 1:1 square cuts for feed posts — 1080×1080
- 4:5 for Facebook/Instagram feed — 1080×1350
- Duration: 15-30 seconds for organic, 6-8 seconds for paid ad cutdowns

## Narrative arc (preferred)

- Open with technology / process (cosmic, build, or data visualization)
- Middle: brand identity and methodology
- Close: brand mark + "Coming Soon to App Store + Google Play"

## Music and sound

- Cinematic, tech-influenced, modern hip-hop or ambient electronic
- Tempo: 70-90 BPM, confident and measured
- AVOID: high-energy hype tracks, casino fanfares, slot-bell sounds

## Voiceover guidelines

- Calm, measured, confident
- Cowboy voice is brand-distinctive — use sparingly for community/
  lifestyle clips, NOT for picks announcements
- Cowboy voice script example (8 seconds): "Well now, looks like the
  patterns are talkin' again. Y'all best mosey on into our group 'fore
  the herd gets too big."

## Required closing elements

- HitMaster ZK6 wordmark
- "Coming Soon" badges for App Store and Google Play
- Lightning bolt motif animation

## Practical AI video production note

A 15-20 second narrative video with multiple distinct scenes is too
much for current AI video tools in one shot. Render as 3 separate
6-7 second clips and edit together. The hardest section (humans +
complex props) should be locked first, then easier opening/closing.

# 10. ENGAGEMENT POSTING TACTICS

## Engagement principles

- **Reply to every comment within the first hour** — algorithm treats
  reply velocity as quality signal
- **First 30 minutes are everything** — schedule posts for when audience
  is online, be present to engage during that window
- **Mix the formats** — don't run question posts every day, audience
  habituates
- **Don't be afraid of low-effort posts** — "drop a 🔥 if you're ready"
  often outperforms carefully crafted posts

## Weekly posting rhythm template

- **Monday**: Hype / results recap
- **Tuesday**: Question post (drives comments)
- **Wednesday**: Signals drop announcement
- **Thursday**: Hot take / methodology spotlight
- **Friday**: Community celebration
- **Saturday**: Community story / testimonial
- **Sunday**: Week-ahead tease

## Pinned comment to pair with most posts

> 👉 Free community: link in profile bio
> 👑 Inner circle ($.99/mo): daily signals first + deeper analysis
> ⚡ App launching soon — App Store + Google Play
>
> Yesterday's report card: [X exact + Y partial] matches across
> [Z] jurisdictions.

The last line is the conversion lever — provides immediate proof before
clicking through.
