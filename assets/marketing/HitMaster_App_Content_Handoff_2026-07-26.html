1 · Positioning & Audience

What HitMaster is (say this): a data intelligence and analytics platform for daily 3-digit draw games — comparable to ESPN Stats, FiveThirtyEight, or Yahoo Finance, but for numerical pattern analysis. The ZK6™ engine analyzes years of public draw data across 39 tracked jurisdiction feeds — spanning 40+ states & provinces (Tri-State and Western Canada feeds each cover multiple states/provinces) — with 76 tracked draws every day and publishes six ranked daily signals per session, then verifies every signal against official observed outcomes — receipts, not promises.

What it is not (never say): a lottery prediction service, a way to win, a gambling product. It sells analysis and verification, not outcomes. No rate claims, no guarantees ever.

The audience

Knowledgeable daily-draw players. They already understand box vs straight, midday vs evening sessions, and state-by-state games. Do not educate down to them — respect their expertise. What wins them: methodology transparency ("here's WHY this signal"), verification receipts (draw-by-draw, state-by-state), and coverage breadth (their state plus dozens of others). What loses them: hype, guru framing, "guaranteed" anything — this audience has seen every scam; the anti-hype, stats-desk voice IS the differentiator.

4 · Compliance for Ad Production (condensed from Brief v2 — brief wins on conflict)

THE RULE THAT GOVERNS EVERYTHING: the classifier reads every image and video frame via OCR. Captions are clean; images leak. Check the frame, not the caption. Both May de-recommendations came from product-UI imagery with real 3-digit numbers and gambling vocabulary visible in the pixels.

Destination decides the rules

Where the ad will runRules
Paid Meta placements / public FB page / boosted postsSTRICT (Tier-1): must pass the Two-Question Filter — NO 3-digit numbers visible in any frame, NO forbidden vocabulary rendered on screen (HITS · STRAIGHT · BOX · PICKS · WIN · LUCKY · BET · JACKPOT · LOTTERY · MIDDAY/EVENING as session labels, etc.). No product-UI screenshots. Use abstract data visuals, hash placeholders (###), brand graphics.
Inside the free group / Pro group / cross-posts from the groupRelaxed (Tier-2/3/4): product UI, real numbers, box/straight vocabulary allowed — audience opted in. Still: no guarantees, no minors, caption variation across cross-posted groups.
App Store / Google Play listingProduct UI with numbers OK (expected by reviewers). Position as data analytics. NOTHING implying guaranteed outcomes.
Non-Meta channels (YouTube/TikTok)Meta classifier doesn't apply, but keep the brand voice (no hype, no guarantees, no casino imagery) — clips get re-shared into Meta surfaces, so default to Tier-1-clean unless produced explicitly for in-group use.
Default posture for THIS ad set: produce every master ad Two-Question-clean (no 3-digit numbers on screen, no forbidden words rendered), so any cut can run anywhere. Then optionally produce in-group variants that show real product UI. A "subscriber exclusive" badge does NOT protect an asset — if numbers are visible, it's group-only.
