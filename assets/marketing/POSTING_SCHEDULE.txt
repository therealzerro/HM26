HITMASTER ZK6 — DAILY POSTING SCHEDULE (v1, 2026-08-02)

THREE SURFACES, THREE CLOCKS
  PRO    utility clock   — earliest possible, always ahead of the draw.
                           They paid for first access; every minute early
                           is the product working.
  FREE   conversion clock— the covered board must land BEFORE the draw it
                           covers, or the gap is theoretical.
  PUBLIC attention clock — masked cut, no draw deadline. Post when cold
                           audiences browse, not when draws happen.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SESSION 1 · 8:30 AM ET — the morning stack (4 handoffs)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  PRO   1. allday_pro      today's board, first access
  PRO   2. midday_pro      ahead of every midday draw
  PRO   3. verify (pro)    yesterday graded, full precision
  FREE  4. verify (free)   yesterday graded, qualitative

  ⚠ ORDER IS THE BRAND LINE. Boards first, receipts after — the posting
  order embodies "Signals first. Receipts after." A paying member should
  never scroll past yesterday to reach today.
  Free opens its day on PROOF. It is the most persuasive thing we make and
  it costs nothing to lead with.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SESSION 2 · 10:30 AM ET — the contrast pair (2 handoffs, FREE room)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FREE  5. allday_free     the full board, everything visible
  FREE  6. midday_free     the same product, digits covered

  ⚠ POST THESE BACK TO BACK, IN THIS ORDER. The juxtaposition IS the pitch:
  total generosity, then the one thing withheld. Split across hours and the
  contrast is lost; reverse them and a member's first impression of the day
  is withholding.
  ⚠ HARD DEADLINE: midday_free must be up before the earliest midday draw
  (~12:30 ET). 10:30 gives ~2 hours of margin.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SESSION 3 · 4:30 PM ET — SAME-DAY MIDDAY VERIFY (conditional, MANUAL)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FREE      verify_midday — `npm run reel:verify-midday` (MKT-62, built
            2026-08-19). Pro at the operator's call (they had the digits).

  ⚠ THIS IS THE HIGHEST-CONVERSION SLOT IN THE SYSTEM. A free member saw
  the covered midday board this morning (in practice ~8:14 AM — the reel
  stamps the real posted_at). The draw happened. This closes that loop the
  same afternoon — the two timestamps are ON SCREEN. Every other verify
  proves the method in the abstract; this one proves the specific board
  they were shown this morning, cover lifted.
  ⚠ CONDITIONAL = only on days the midday ledger is imported + graded in
  time AND today's midday board has ≥1 match (~70% of days). NEVER in
  reel:daily, never scheduled — run by hand. Free room only; no public cut.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SESSION 4 · 5:30 PM ET — the evening pair (2 handoffs)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  PRO   7. evening_pro     ahead of every evening draw
  FREE  8. evening_free    digits covered, ahead of the draw

  ⚠ HARD DEADLINE: before the earliest evening draw (~6:30 ET). 5:30 is
  safer than 5:45 — some jurisdictions cut earlier than you'd expect.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SESSION 5 · 7:00 PM ET — public (1 handoff)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  PUBLIC  9. ONE cut, ALTERNATING DAILY: allday_public / verify_public
          → YouTube Shorts + cross-post, same time every day.

  ⚠ ONE PER DAY, NOT TWO. Two public cuts daily was flagged as a cadence
  risk at registration and it still is. Alternating gives one consistent
  slot, more variety across the week, and half the repetition risk on a
  channel with no history.
  ⚠ 7 PM BECAUSE THE PUBLIC CUT HAS NO DEADLINE. It is masked — nothing in
  it expires at a draw time. So it should post when strangers scroll, which
  is evening, not when the board was generated.

WHAT THIS FIXES IN THE CURRENT ORDER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  · free allday landed AFTER free midday, so the gap arrived before the

    rewards a consistent daily slot had none.

    reads boards-then-receipts.

  1. Public (evergreen — tomorrow is fine)
  2. Free All-Day (habit, not urgency)
  3. Verify (proof keeps)
  NEVER SKIP: any board ahead of its own draw. Pro paid for that, and
