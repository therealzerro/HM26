1. WHAT THE SYSTEM IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A fully automated daily video pipeline. Two commands produce, assemble, upload,
and stage everything; the operator posts from their phone with two taps.

  npm run reel:allday   → TWO reels for TODAY (Pro variant + Free variant),
                          23.7s each: the All-Day signal board + all six
                          signal detail modals, captured from the LIVE app
                          with real data.

  npm run reel:verify   → ONE reel for YESTERDAY (10.0s): "Receipts" —
                          yesterday's verification breakdown. Intentionally
                          refuses to build on zero-match days (no receipts,
                          no reel).

Each run ends by auto-uploading the finals to the app backend. They appear in
the operator's Admin → Reels screen seconds later with a fresh, auto-written
caption (see §5). NOTHING auto-posts to Facebook — a human always completes
the post (platform rule + brand-safety rule).

Everything is captured from the real product UI — no mockups, no comps. The
renderer drives the actual app, verifies each signal modal is the right one,
and runs an in-frame vocabulary guard; any violation aborts the render.

2. REEL ANATOMY — ALL-DAY (Pro + Free, 23.7s @ 1080×1920 60fps)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both variants share one UI body render; they differ in carrier (voiceover) and
endcard branding.

  TIMELINE (t in seconds)
  0.0 – 1.2    OPEN — settled brand lockup (pulled from the endcard's final
               frame), 1.2s eased dissolve into the UI.
  1.2 – 17.2   BODY (16.0s) — 4.0s slow push-in over the full 2×3 signal
               grid (all six on screen), then each signal tapped in rank
               order 1→6: modal opens, ~2.0s hold on digits/energy/
               confidence/breakdown, close. Hard cuts between modals.
  17.2 – 23.7  OUTRO (6.5s) — the endcard's FIRST 6.5s, hard cut in:
               smoke rise → bolt snap (~18.4s, its crack lands exactly on
               the snap) → lockup resolves → calm hold.

  AUDIO (two automatic modes, loudness −14 LUFS integrated):
  - LONG CARRIER (VO ≥ ~17.2s) — "overlap mode": the voice plays wall-to-
    wall over open+body and may ride up to 1.1s into the smoke rise, but
    must finish before the 18.4s bolt snap. The endcard's own synced outro
    audio is mixed in from 17.2s. Carrier content past the VO is DISCARDED
    (generated tracks are unreliable after their voice ends).
  - SHORT CARRIER (VO ~10s) — the voice covers the grid + first modals;
    the endcard's hum swell (its audio after the crack) is pulled forward
    as a low bed under the remaining modals, then the endcard plays its
    outro natively.

  WHAT THIS MEANS FOR CARRIER PRODUCTION (your main deliverable):
  - Target VO length: ~17.2s for wall-to-wall narration (preferred), or
    ~10s accepting the hum-bed remainder. Anything between works; the
    assembler adapts automatically and prints which mode it used.
  - Write VO to the visual beat map: 0–1.2 brand open → 1.2–5.2 the full
    board (six signals on screen) → 5.2 onward, one signal detail roughly
    every 2s → if long-form, final line can land over the smoke rise.
  - Never script the VO to reference the bolt snap or anything after it —
    that space belongs to the endcard's synced sound.
  - Two variants every time: PRO (first-access framing, never pricing) and
    FREE (pure value — the All-Day drop carries NO Pro pitch, per the
    SOCIAL-13 depth rule).

  ENDCARD CONTRACT (full spec in the endcard revision brief):
  - Final frame must be the settled lockup (it is reused as the reel's
    opening frame — the loop is intentional).
  - First 6.5s must be the formation sequence (used as the outro).
  - Audio must be front-loaded and synced to the visual snap; the
    assembler hard-aborts if endcard audio is too short for the outro/bed.

3. REEL ANATOMY — VERIFY / "RECEIPTS" (10.0s @ 1080×1920 60fps)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.0 – 1.2    OPEN — settled bolt lockup (from verif_endcard's tail),
               1.2s eased dissolve into the UI.
  1.2 – 7.5    BODY (6.3s) — yesterday's verification breakdown captured
               from the live app.
  7.5 – 10.0   CLOSE — final 2.5s of verif_endcard (static lockup), hard cut.
  AUDIO        verif_carrier's track across the full 10.0s, −14 LUFS,
               de-click edge fades. No other sound.

  - Builds for YESTERDAY by default. If yesterday had zero verified
    matches, the pipeline aborts on purpose — we never fake receipts.

4. AFTER ASSEMBLY — THE AUTOMATIC PUBLISH CHAIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per reel, three finals are produced and uploaded (working files stay local):
  - 9:16 master        e.g. allday_pro_20260727.mp4 / verify_reel_20260726.mp4
  - 1:1 feed cut       …_1x1.mp4 (center crop)
  - contact sheet PNG  …_contact.png (the storyboard strip)

Upload target: public storage (bucket "marketing-reels"), registered in the
app's database one row per (date, kind). kind ∈ allday_pro | allday_free |
verify. Storage self-prunes after 30 days (the log rows are kept).

The operator's Admin → Reels screen then offers, per reel:
  - contact-sheet storyboard preview + buttons to watch the 9:16 / 1:1
  - target selector: Free Group / Pro Group / Cross-Post
  - the auto-written caption in an editor, gated by the full brand-lint
    engine (a blocking vocabulary violation disables the send buttons)
  - Two-Question NO/NO checkboxes, required for cross-posts (reels show
    real digits, so cross-posts honestly fail Q1 — that is the filter
    working; reels live in the groups)
  - posting flows: phone = Save to Photos + Open Group (attach from camera
    roll inside Facebook — the only reliable FB video path); web =
    Prepare Video, then Save/Share (native sheet) or Download
  - every handoff is logged centrally with same-day duplicate-caption
    detection across all surfaces

There is deliberately NO public-page (API) lane for reels and NO auto-posting.

5. THE CAPTION SYSTEM (matters for any copy you propose)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Captions are generated at publish time by a template engine — not written
per-day by hand, not AI-freestyled.

  ROTATION — each content kind has 8 template families. The day's date picks
  the template deterministically: a different style every day, never the
  same style on two kinds the same day (protects the duplicate-caption
  rule), and re-running the same date reproduces the same caption exactly.

  THE NUMBERS POLICY (operator ruling, absolute):
  - PRO captions ONLY may contain real performance data: verified counts,
    state attributions, STRAIGHT MATCH callouts, 30-day totals. These are
    computed fresh from the verified results record (yesterday's board
    checked against actual observed draws — never cached flags).
  - FREE captions are QUALITATIVE. They may DESCRIBE matches creatively,
    but only in descriptor language — scale words ("a verified MATCH" /
    "multiple verified MATCHES" / "a stack of verified MATCHES"), spread
    words ("across several state boards" / "from coast to coast"), and
    STRAIGHT MATCH presence ("one of them a dead-on STRAIGHT MATCH").
    Never counts, never state names. The video shows the receipts; the
    caption text a classifier reads stays clean. Gambling-adjacency
    protection — do not ask for exceptions.
  - THE VERIFY REEL CARRIES TWO DRAFTS (one reel, two rooms): a free-group
    draft (qualitative description, above) and a pro-group draft (full
    precision). In the operator's Reels screen, switching the target
    between Free and Pro loads the matching draft automatically.
    Example, same day (7/24):
      FREE: "Grade day: 7/24 closed with a stack of verified MATCHES from
             coast to coast — one of them a dead-on STRAIGHT MATCH. Pull
             your state's results and compare."
      PRO:  "Graded in full: 7 MATCHES across 7 states on 7/24 — including
             a STRAIGHT MATCH in PA. 🧾"

  VOICE RULES for any template you propose:
  - The product noun is "signals". Always. (Never picks/plays/numbers.)
  - Match vocabulary: MATCH / BOX MATCH / STRAIGHT MATCH. Never "hit(s)",
    never "partial match", never win/won/winning.
  - No guarantees, no urgency hype ("don't miss out", "act now"), max ~2
    emoji, viewer-friendly plain language — receipts-and-record energy,
    not hype.
  - Free All-Day captions never pitch Pro. Pro captions never mention
    pricing.
  - Approved coverage claims: "40+ states & provinces" or "39 tracked
    feeds" (never "40+ jurisdictions").

  PROPOSING NEW TEMPLATES: deliver lines as text following the rules above,
  tagged by kind (pro / free / verify). Engineering adds them to the
  registry — template count per kind can grow beyond 8; rotation adapts.

  New content types (future reels/posts) get their own registry entry with
  the same contract. The realNumbers flag is structural: a non-pro template
  physically cannot access performance data.

6. HARD CONSTRAINTS (non-negotiable, from the brand brief)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The Facebook page is under algorithmic probation (de-recommended twice,
    appeals denied). Public page = brand-only; reels and their captions are
    GROUP content (free = conversion funnel, pro = paid room).
  - Two-Question filter is mandatory before anything leaves surfaces we
    control (cross-posts): Q1 no 3-digit numbers visible, Q2 no forbidden
    vocabulary rendered/spoken. Both must be NO.
  - Pro group price is $2.49/mo where pricing is allowed (free-group copy);
    NO app subscription price in any ad or caption.
  - The in-frame vocabulary guard and the caption lint are enforcement, not
    suggestions — plan assets so they pass, don't plan appeals.
