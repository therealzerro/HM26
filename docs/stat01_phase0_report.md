## 0.6 FEASIBILITY
Rows: 465 boards · 12,178 draws (≈ 11,900 in the board era) · 2,789 picks. Vectorised design: precompute `counts[board_day, 220 classes]` (draws in the board's graded pool per box class) and `counts_ord[board_day, 1000]` for straights; a composition-matched replacement is 6 class indices sampled without replacement within stratum; S*_r = one gather-sum. R = 10,000 → ~28 M gathers ≈ **seconds**; R = 100,000 ≈ **under 2 minutes** in numpy; the three sanity nulls (100 harness runs of (a)) ≈ minutes. **Not hours.** Use R = 100,000 and report both.

## §2 ARITHMETIC — RE-DERIVED (D12)
Exhaustive enumeration: 1,000 ordered outcomes → **220 box classes = 120 six-way (6 orderings) + 90 doubles (3) + 10 triples (1); 720 + 270 + 10 = 1,000 ✓.** Disjointness of distinct classes vs one draw ✓, so per-draw board probability is the exact sum. With the agent's inputs (6 six-way, 70 draws): 0.036/draw · E 2.520/day · P(≥1) 0.9232 · 27.7 of 30 days · 75.6/30d (sd 8.69) · straights 0.420/day, P(≥1) 0.3438, 10.3/30d — **all reproduce.** Uniform-over-220 mean 1/220 = 0.004545; six-way lift 1.32× ✓. Two caveats, both in the agent's favour of caution: (i) `1−(1−p)^n` across draws assumes independence across draw events — the composition-matched permutation handles this, the closed form is Phase 2's "crude" number only; (ii) **70 is wrong as the input**: actual pools are 32.2 (midday), 41.5 (evening), 73.7 (allday) — the All-Day figure alone is close to 70, the three-board pool is not. The agent's arithmetic is right; the scope it was applied to was ambiguous, exactly as §0 suspected.
